# Intellectual Property and Clean-Room Policy

Status: binding owner policy for every contributor and AI agent working in this repository.
Adopted 2026-09-27. It takes precedence over any task instruction that conflicts with it.

The goal is to build original work, respect other people's rights, and keep legal questions
with the owner and qualified counsel.

## Original work only

- Describe and design features in this project's own terms. Do not frame the project's scope
  as matching, cloning or reaching parity with a named product.
- Do not read, copy, translate, port or closely paraphrase another product's source code,
  documentation text, test suites, grammars, examples or visual designs to implement this
  project. Ideas and features common to many products are fine; their expression is not.
- Code, text or data from Q&A sites, blogs, tutorials or AI output counts as third-party
  material. Use it only if its license allows, with origin and attribution recorded, or
  write the part independently.
- If generated output reproduces a recognizable, substantial piece of existing third-party
  code or text, discard it and write that part independently.

## Licensed material and dependencies

- Use dependencies, specifications, standards, fonts, icons, datasets and model weights only
  under licenses that permit the intended use. Keep required notices and record origin,
  version and license.
- Where a license restricts AI or computational use, as some standards documents do, do not
  give that material to an AI agent. A maintainer writes a feature summary in the project's
  own words instead, unless a separate license expressly permits AI-assisted use.
- Do not add a dependency whose license conflicts with this project's license without a
  recorded owner decision.

## Patents

- Do not search for, read, analyze, cite or link third-party patents or patent applications.
- If a specific patent or other IP concern comes to your attention, stop that item, report
  it privately to the owner and continue with other work. Never record patent numbers or
  legal analysis in Git, commit messages, issues, pull requests, prompts or public discussion.
- Freedom-to-operate and other legal review is done by qualified counsel outside the
  repository. Nothing in this repository states or implies a legal conclusion.

## Names and trademarks

- Do not use other companies' trademarks in product names, logos or branding.
- Compatibility claims follow the trademark owner's published policy and need evidence.
- Publish benchmarks or comparisons against a third-party product only where that product's
  license permits publishing results.

## Host privacy

Keep private host paths, usernames, hostnames, credentials and personal data out of committed
files. Use repository-relative paths or placeholders such as `<repo>` and `<home>`.

## When unsure

Stop the uncertain item, report it privately to the owner and continue with unrelated work.
Uncertainty about IP is never a reason to guess, copy or commit.
